WEKA IMPLEMENTATION – STEP BY STEP
=====================================

1. Open WEKA.
2. Select Explorer.
3. In the Preprocess tab, click Open file.
4. Select online_shopping_behaviour.arff.
5. Check that 200 instances and 12 attributes are loaded.
6. In the Class dropdown at the bottom, select Purchase.
7. Open the Classify tab.
8. Click Choose > trees > J48.
9. Under Test options, select Cross-validation and keep 10 folds.
10. Click Start. Save a screenshot of the output.
11. Repeat with Choose > bayes > NaiveBayes.
12. Repeat with Choose > trees > RandomForest.
13. Record each algorithm's correctly classified instances, accuracy,
    precision, recall, F-measure, and confusion matrix.
14. Compare the three algorithms in a table in your report.

Optional visual exploration:
- Open the Visualize tab to inspect attribute distributions and scatter plots.
- In Preprocess, select attributes one by one and use Visualize All to explore
  customer patterns.
- You can also use Filter > unsupervised > attribute > Discretize for
  experimenting with numeric attributes, but keep the original dataset for
  the main comparison.

How to explain the project:
"The project analyses shopping-session information such as time spent,
pages viewed, items added to cart, visitor type and previous purchases.
WEKA classification algorithms learn patterns from these attributes and
predict the Purchase class as Yes or No. We compare J48, Naive Bayes and
Random Forest using 10-fold cross-validation."

Note:
Do not assume an accuracy in advance. Use the values shown by your own WEKA run.
